Performance & Discipline Situations S-04
S-04 Harassment / Protected-Class Allegation
Tier 2 — Escalate to HR ▼
  • 1Treat all allegations seriously — even informal, offhand, or "don't report this" comments trigger mandatory escalation.
  • 2Listen attentively without offering personal opinions, promises of secrecy, or defensive arguments.
  • 3State required duty: "I take this seriously. Under company policy, I must report this immediately to HR so it can be reviewed objectively."
  • 4Report details to HR immediately.
⛔ Hard Stop — No Independent Investigation
Do not conduct an independent investigation or interview the accused parties yourself. Report to HR immediately and let an authorized investigator take it from there.
