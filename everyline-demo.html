01 Thornfield Direct Contact Center
✉ Before — sticky note on the monitor
"if someone wants to CANCEL — do NOT just cancel!! read them the retention thing first, it's in the folder somewhere. ask Denise if u can't find it"
Source: handwritten, taped to Agent #14's monitor, undated
✓ After — Everyline℠ Console
Case Type Identification
Cancellation Request — Retention Offer Required
Step Sequence
1. Confirm the request. 2. Deliver the retention disclosure verbatim. 3. Log the customer's response. 4. Process cancellation only after disclosure is complete.
Agent Script
"Before I process that, I'm required to walk you through your account options first — it'll take one minute."
Hard Stop
Do not cancel before the retention disclosure is delivered and logged.
Escalation Trigger
Customer disputes being charged after a stated cancellation date — route to Billing Resolution.
02 Thornfield Retail Retail
📄 Before — outdated PDF, page 14 of 40
"...associates should exercise discretion regarding returns without proof of purchase, subject to store manager approval when applicable, per policy dated [illegible]..."
Source: "Store_Handbook_FINAL_v3_USE THIS ONE.pdf", last edited 4 years ago
✓ After — Everyline℠ Console
Case Type Identification
Return — No Proof of Purchase
Step Sequence
1. Check for a receipt or digital purchase record. 2. If none exists, offer store-credit-only exchange per posted policy. 3. Document the transaction, including any manager override.
Agent Script
"Without a receipt, I can offer store credit at today's price — would that work for you?"
Hard Stop
No cash refund without a verifiable proof of purchase.
Escalation Trigger
Customer requests exception above posted return policy limits — route to Store Manager for override authority.
03 The Thornfield Inn Hospitality
💬 Before — front-desk group chat
"hey does anyone know the actual rule for service animals at check in?? guest is asking and I don't wanna say the wrong thing 😅"

"lol just don't ask for papers, I think that's the main thing"
Source: team messaging app, front-desk channel
✓ After — Everyline℠ Console
Case Type Identification
Guest Service Animal Accommodation — Check-In
Step Sequence
1. Ask only the two permitted questions. 2. Do not request documentation or a demonstration. 3. Confirm the room assignment accommodates the animal. 4. Log the accommodation, not the guest's disability.
Agent Script
"Is that a service animal required because of a disability, and what work or task is it trained to perform?"
Hard Stop
Do not ask for documentation, certification, or a demonstration of the animal's training (ADA Title III).
Escalation Trigger
Guest is denied accommodation by any staff member — route immediately to Property Manager, same shift.
04 Thornfield Claims Insurance Claims
💬 Before — "just ask someone who's been here"
New adjusters are told: "if a claimant pushes back on a denial, just document it and let it sit — someone will get back to it eventually." No one has ever mentioned a required response window.
Source: informal onboarding, no written record
✓ After — Everyline℠ Console
Case Type Identification
Claim Denial Disputed by Claimant
Step Sequence
1. Log the dispute with date and time received. 2. Acknowledge receipt to the claimant in writing within the required window. 3. Route the file for review. 4. Track the response deadline — do not let it lapse.
Agent Script
"I've logged your dispute today. You'll receive a written response within the timeframe required by state regulation."
Hard Stop
A disputed claim cannot sit without a logged acknowledgment date — required response windows are regulatory, not discretionary (state Unfair Claims Settlement Practices Act / NAIC Model #900).
Escalation Trigger
Response deadline is within 48 hours and file review is incomplete — escalate to Claims Supervisor immediately.
